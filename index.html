About Shree Gau Seva Dham

A Lifelong Sanctuary Built on Devotion & Compassion

In Indian culture, the cow is revered as Gomata—a mother and symbol of unconditional nurturing. Shree Gau Seva Dham was established to give helpless, non-milking, injured, and abandoned cows an honorable, loving home.

Unlike commercial dairies, not a single drop of milk is sold here. Every cow, calf, and aged bull spends its entire natural life in tranquility, receiving pure nutrition, clean shelter, daily prayers, and dedicated medical treatment.

Comprehensive Seva

What We Do for Gomata

Every single service is executed with love, dignity, and religious adherence to Vedic traditions.

Daily Fodder & Nutrition

Providing high-grade green grass, dry hay, mustard oil-cakes, fortified wheat bran, and mineral licks tailored for digestion.

Advanced Veterinary Care

Resident veterinary doctors performing wound dressing, fracture immobilization, vaccinations, and surgical procedures around the clock.

24/7 Cattle Ambulance

Hydraulic lift-enabled cow ambulances ready on a single helpline call to rescue road-accident and stranded victims in the region.

Special Care for Calves

Dedicated nursery ward for newborn motherless calves, providing warm milk formula, hygiene blankets, and gentle human affection.

Open Pasture Grazing

Over 15 acres of natural grassland where cows freely socialize, bask under morning sun, and graze in an unconfined environment.

Gau Seva & Havan Yagya

Promoting the spiritual values of Gau Seva through community havan, educational visits for students, and organic Panchagavya awareness.

Our Sacred Duty
